About EveryDay Clinic

Patient-centered outpatient care designed for clarity, comfort, and timely scheduling.

EveryDay Clinic brings together experienced general practitioners and visiting specialists under one roof. Our online appointment prototype reduces phone congestion and helps patients plan visits with clear time slots and upfront consultation fees.

Our values

Customer Safety

Strict safety standards, qualified staff and privacy patient.

Value time

Appointments start on time and online booking means no queues.

Always flexible

Cancel or reschedule whenever plans change.
